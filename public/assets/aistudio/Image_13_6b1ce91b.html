Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, channel limits, and platform security.

Can I switch between monthly and annual plans anytime?

expand_more
Yes! You can upgrade, downgrade, or switch billing frequency directly within your Account Settings. If you switch from monthly to annual mid-cycle, any remaining prorated balance is credited automatically against your annual invoice.

How does Instagram and YouTube API rate limit protection work?

expand_more
ConnectStream deploys an intelligent pacing engine that monitors real-time quota telemetry on both the Instagram Graph API and YouTube Data v3 API. If Meta or Google throttle request speeds, our system queues and staggers your publishing bursts without failing your scheduled time slots.

What happens if I exceed my scheduled post quota or storage?

expand_more
You will receive a notification at 80% and 95% capacity. We never cancel your scheduled uploads unexpectedly. If you exceed limits, you can either upgrade instantly to Pro/Agency with one click or add a flexible high-capacity storage pack for $5/month per 50GB.

Is there a free trial without credit card required?

expand_more
Yes, our 14-day free trial on Starter and Pro Studio tiers gives you immediate access to test automated dispatch and video transcoding without entering any payment credentials upfront. You only choose a plan when your trial completes.

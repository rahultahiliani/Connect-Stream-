## Brand & Style

This design system targets modern digital creators, video producers, and cross-platform growth managers orchestrating presence across visual video ecosystems. It reflects an energetic, futuristic, high-performance ethos where data mastery converges with creative media distribution.

The style unites futuristic dark-mode aesthetics with selective glassmorphism and radiant cybernetic luminescence:
- **Base Canvas:** Deep obsidian and midnight navy tones create an infinite, low-glare digital studio canvas.
- **Illumination Model:** Vibrant violet and electric cyan neon accents evoke terminal consoles, dynamic pulse lines, and glowing broadcast indicators.
- **Surface Quality:** Frosted glass panels layered with subtle translucent borders (`1px` border with low-opacity gradient or white trace) to deliver depth without visual noise.
- **Emotional Resonance:** High agency, futuristic efficiency, precision control, and cinematic vibrancy.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-deep dark environment with neon spectral highlights that separate content streams, platform identities, and performance metrics:

### Primary & Accent Spectrum
- **Primary Violet (`#8B5CF6`, `#7C3AED`):** Signals main interactive flows, core brand elements, primary CTA states, and active channel focus.
- **Secondary Neon Cyan (`#06B6D4`, `#3B82F6`):** Highlights positive metrics, real-time data sync indicators, charts, and secondary callouts.
- **Tertiary Magenta / Pink (`#EC4899`):** Reserved for high-priority alerts, Instagram-native metrics, viral momentum markers, and critical actions.

### Canvas & Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#090D16`):** Deepest obsidian background, providing total immersion and contrast.
- **Surface Container Base (`#0D111E`):** Distinct layout regions, sidebars, and header bars.
- **Surface Container Elevated (`#141A29`):** Primary card bodies, metric containers, modal sheets, and data widgets.
- **Surface Glass / Interactive (`rgba(20, 26, 41, 0.65)`): Backdrop-filtered panels with translucent border glows.

### Platform Identifiers
- **Instagram Neon:** Radial gradient blend (`#833AB4` to `#FD1D1D` to `#F56040`).
- **YouTube Vivid Red:** `#FF0000` with subtle ambient red-glow boundary.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes rapid scannability and structural precision. `Plus Jakarta Sans` delivers geometric authority for hero sections, section titles, and analytical metric figures, while `Inter` provides neutral clarity for compact metadata, platform status chips, and tabular values.

Numbers and metric counters must always utilize tabular figures (`tnum`) to eliminate layout jitter during live stream syncs and rapid metric updates. Upper-case labeling is paired with subtle letter-spacing (`+0.05em`) on small tags to preserve legibility against deep dark backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt spatial grid configured for high-density creator workflows:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Fixed collateral navigation sidebar (`240px`), fluid main operational canvas with a 12-column responsive layout, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Collapsed icon-only sidebar (`72px`), fluid 8-column layout, and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column vertical stack with persistent glass bottom navigation bar (`64px` height), `0.75rem` (12px) gutters, and `1rem` (16px) margins.

Grid cards stretch to fill modular multi-column blocks (e.g., metric cards span 3 columns each on desktop, media previews span 2 or 3 columns, and deep analytics charts span 6 to 12 columns).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on frosted glass layers, translucent edges, and ambient neon back-glow rather than standard dark drop shadows:

1. **Base Layer (Level 0):** Pure `#090D16` matte canvas.
2. **Structural Glass (Level 1):** `#0D111E` at `80%` opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` perimeter stroke.
3. **Floating Widgets & Cards (Level 2):** `#141A29` at `90%` opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, `1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)` stroke, and an ambient tinted drop shadow: `0 8px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
4. **Active & Hover Glows (Level 3):** Interactive cards elevate with an exterior illumination aura: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.35)` and boundary stroke `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.4)`.
5. **Overlays & Modals (Level 4):** Deep obsidian modal backdrop at `70%` blur, dialog container at `95%` opacity, bordered with dual neon gradient trace (`#8B5CF6` fading to `#06B6D4`).

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry to balance sleek technological precision with modern digital approachability:
- Standard interactive elements (input fields, dropdowns, list rows) maintain an outer radius of `0.5rem` (8px).
- Widget surfaces, media card frames, and modal cards adopt `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Status chips, navigation pills, and avatar badges use true pill geometry (`9999px`).
- Media thumbnails within the asset grid inherit an inner radius of `0.5rem` (8px) within the enclosing card.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Neon CTA:** Gradient fill transitioning from `#7C3AED` to `#6366F1`, white text (`label-lg`), subtle inner top highlight (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), and ambient purple shadow (`0 4px 16px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Glass Action:** Background `rgba(20, 26, 41, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, hover state introduces cyan neon border (`#06B6D4`) and subtle surface lightning.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent background, muted text (`#94A3B8`), hover reveals `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill.

### Metric Widgets & Analytics Cards
- Card surface at `Level 2` elevation with subtle top gradient border highlight.
- Header row features a small platform indicator badge (Instagram / YouTube pill) and period toggle.
- Metrics rendered in `metric-display` scale alongside delta badges (+128% in neon cyan `#06B6D4` pill).
- Interactive sparklines and area charts rendered with gradient fills fading from neon cyan or violet to transparent.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Filter chips sit inside a segmented bar with a unified backdrop (`#0D111E`).
- Active pill switches to solid `#7C3AED` or high-contrast cyan with white text.
- Inactive pills maintain transparent fills with `#94A3B8` typography and hover brightening.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#0D111E` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` border.
- Focus state activates an electric purple perimeter glow: `border-color: #8B5CF6`, `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2)`.
- Icon prefixes (search glass, platform toggles) rendered in muted neon tones.

### Media Library Grid Cards
- 16:9 and 9:16 aspect containers with overlay status tags (Reel, Short, Story, Video) anchored top-left.
- Duration tags anchored bottom-right in high-contrast frosted black pills (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`).
- Hover trigger displays an active glowing violet border ring with quick-action overlays (Schedule, Edit, Analytics).

### Platform Status Connectors
- Dedicated connection cards for Instagram and YouTube.
- Green status pulse indicator dot (`#10B981`) for synced platforms with active account handles.
- Action buttons toggle between "Connected" and "Manage" states with high-contrast pill styling.
